Jason A. Heppler writes about the environmental history of the North American West and Great Plains, political economy, technology, and the politics of land.

He is senior developer-scholar at the Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media and Adjunct Professor of History at George Mason University, and an Affiliate Fellow at the Center for Great Plains Studies at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln.

He is the author of Silicon Valley and the Environmental Inequalities of High-Tech Urbanism, which the LA Review of Books called “a subversive view of Silicon Valley’s history,” and co-editor of Digital Community Engagement: Partnering Communities with the Academy, winner of the National Council on Public History's 2021 book award.

He has written for the Washington Post and The Conversation, among others.
