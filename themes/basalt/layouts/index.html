Jason A. Heppler is a historian of the North American West and Great Plains, and a builder of digital history. He is the author of Silicon Valley and the Environmental Inequalities of High-Tech Urbanism, and serves as senior developer/scholar at the Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media and Affiliate Fellow at the Center for Great Plains Studies.
